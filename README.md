# HR-Data-Analytical-Dashboard-Excel-
An interactive Excel Dashboard that analyses employee records.

Key Metrics
Total employees -1,470
Total attrition -237
Attrition rate -16.12%
Average age -36.92
Average monthly income -$6,502.93

Dashboard Features
Slicers: Job Role, Department, Gender and Attrition filter every chart at once.
Visuals: employee distribution by job role, gender, department, education level, age category and income category.
Pivot-based: all charts are driven by PivotTables built from the cleaned dataset.

Key Insights
Attrition: 237 of 1,470 employees left, an attrition rate of 16.12%, which is the main retention concern for HR.
Department mix: Research & Development is the largest department (961 employees, about 65%), followed by Sales (446, about 30%) and Human Resources (63, about 4%).
Job roles: Sales Executive (326), Research Scientist (291) and Laboratory Technician (260) are the three biggest roles, together making up about 60% of the workforce. Human Resources is the smallest role (52).
Gender: The workforce is 60% male (882) and 40% female (588).
Education: Most employees hold a Bachelor's degree (572), followed by a Master's (398) and some university (282). Only 48 hold a Doctorate.
Age: Adults make up the majority of staff at 55% (811), with an average age of 36.92.
Income: The average monthly income is $6,502.93.

Recommendations
Use the Attrition and Job Role slicers to find which roles and departments lose the most staff, then target retention efforts there.
Review gender balance in hiring, especially in the larger roles.
Compare income against attrition to check whether pay is driving people to leave.

Tools & Skills
Microsoft Excel, PivotTables, slicers, VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, data cleaning, dashboard design.
